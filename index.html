Our mission

Recovery Needs More Than a Bed

Domus Sobrietatis Ministry Trust is building a stable environment where veterans can restore healthy routines, strengthen community ties, and move forward with dignity.

Structure, fellowship, and a path forward

Many veterans leave treatment or unstable circumstances without a dependable place to continue their progress. Our housing model is designed to bridge that gap with clear expectations, sober living, shared responsibility, and practical support.

Residents are encouraged to build consistency through household participation, recovery-focused activities, mentorship, service, and healthy community involvement.

Our belief: lasting change grows where stability, accountability, purpose, and community are present every day.

Program structure

Clear Standards Create Stability

Consistency and accountability help residents establish routines that support long-term recovery.

  • Drug- and alcohol-free living environment
  • Curfew: 10:00 PM weekdays and 11:00 PM weekends
  • Weekly house meetings and consistent accountability
  • Participation in recovery-focused programming
  • Shared chores and respect for the home environment
  • Ongoing focus on responsibility, personal growth, and community participation

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

General information about housing, referrals, and community support.

Who may submit a housing inquiry?

Veterans, family members, case managers, treatment providers, and referral partners may contact us. Final placement depends on program fit, readiness, and availability.

Is the home drug and alcohol free?

Yes. Residents are expected to maintain sobriety and follow all house rules and recovery-focused expectations.

Can organizations refer veterans?

Yes. Treatment providers, courts, veteran organizations, churches, and case managers are encouraged to contact us regarding referrals and partnerships.

How can the community help?

Support can include financial contributions, household supplies, transportation assistance, mentorship, professional services, and volunteer projects.
